# Dose Bar v6: assembly

Tools: a 2.5 mm hex key (or the driver that fits your M3 screws). No glue, no springs.

## Screws (per head)

| Qty | Screw | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | M3 × 10 (8–12 works) | Funnel insert down into the chassis |
| 3 | M3 × 12 (10 works) | Guide plate onto the hub |
| 1 | M3 × 8 (8–10 works) | Dial, from under the slide |
| 1 | M3 × 8 | Agitator gate onto its pin, from inside the funnel |

All screws cut their own thread in PLA. Stop when snug.

## Steps

**1. Slide** (work with the slide right way up: dial well on top)
1. Drop the **plunger** into the slot; its rack rod goes into the channel. Push it fully back so the chamber is closed.
2. Drop the **dial** into the well with **0 at the triangle mark**.
3. Fit the **M3 × 8** from underneath. Snug, then back off a quarter turn so the dial turns freely.
4. Drop the **pawl** into its pocket, nose toward the dial, then the **TPU spring** tube behind it.
5. Turn the dial to the top of its range and back to 0. Check: 48 even clicks per turn, the plunger follows, and at 0 the plunger touches the chamber's back wall.
6. Slide the **name band** into the dovetail across the face.

**2. Chassis**
1. Push a **stop boot** over each of the two stop piers, and the **bumper** over the block at the back (its soft tube points forward).

**3. Funnel insert** (turn it upside down)
1. Press the **U-seal** root into its groove. The thin blade must lean **toward the throat opening**.
2. From the outside, push the **wand arm**'s pin through the hole in the funnel wall.
3. Inside the funnel, put the **gate** on the pin's flat and fix it with the **M3 × 8**.
4. Push the **TPU finger** through the square socket at the arm's lower end, from the outside, until its head seats.

**4. Head**
1. Lay the slide on the chassis floor between the rails and push it back until the bumper's tube clicks into the slot in its rear end.
2. Lower the insert onto the chassis walls. The arm drops through the notch in the plate, and the finger's tip goes into the long notch in the slide's side.
3. Fix with **6 × M3 × 10**.
4. Pull the slide out by its face and push it back a few times. It should run freely, stop softly at both ends, and the gate should rock at the end of each stroke.
5. Drop the **shell** over the insert.
6. Drop the **ring** on, grooves up.
7. Fit a **boot** on each **jaw**. Push the three jaws into the guide plate's tracks from the middle.
8. Turn the ring so the groove ends sit under the tracks, lower the plate so each jaw pin enters its groove, and fix with **3 × M3 × 12**.
9. Turn the ring: all three jaws must move together. Counter-clockwise seen from above closes them.

**5. Base**
1. Slide a **latch** into the dovetail in each front corner of the **scoop** (either way round).
2. Put the **tub** on its **mat** tile, the scoop in the tub, and the head on the tub. Push the scoop in until it is flush.

**6. Jar**
1. Fill the jar. Lay the **gasket** on its rim, lips down toward the glass.
2. Turn the head upside down over the jar. Twist the ring **clockwise** (like a lid) until the jaws are snug on the neck. Do not force it.
3. Hold the head, turn the whole thing over, and set it on the tub.

Always **carry it by the base**, not by the jar: the chuck centers and seals the jar, it is not a handle.

## Row

Mat tiles lock together with the dovetail tabs on their sides. They set an even 6 mm gap between heads and keep the row from sliding when you pull a scoop.
